# Step 5: get to know the business

Goal: short, accurate notes in `context/` so your suggestions and drafts are specific to this business. Work most of it out yourself; ask as little as possible.

## 5.1 Work it out first

With the connections from steps 2 to 4, quietly gather:
- From their **sent email** (last 2 to 3 weeks): who they deal with most, their clients and suppliers, how they write.
- From their **calendar**: their working pattern and regular meetings.
- From **calls**: what they sell and what's in progress.
- From their **website** (from their email signature), if you can read web pages: what the business does.
- From **accounts or CRM**: key customers.

Don't present guesses as facts. Use them to skip questions.

## 5.2 Learn how they write

Ask once:

> Can I read 15 or so of your recent sent emails to learn how you write? Then my drafts will sound like you. I'll keep notes on your style, not copies of the emails.

If yes, follow `.claude/skills/write-like-me` in learn mode.

## 5.3 Fill in the notes

| File | What goes in |
|---|---|
| `context/business.md` | What the business does, for whom, what it sells, location, website |
| `context/people.md` | Team and helpers, key clients and suppliers, and how formal to be with each |
| `context/priorities.md` | Ask one question: "What matters most over the next 3 months?" |
| `context/routines.md` | Working pattern, plus anything recurring you spotted (VAT, payroll, monthly invoicing). Ask: "Anything else you do every week or month that I should remind you about?" |
| `context/voice.md` | From 5.2 |

Change each file's status line to `Status: filled in on <date>`.

Also create the folders you'll need under `files/` (for example a folder per key client in `files/02-clients/`). If their documents live in Google Drive or OneDrive, fill in the "Where things really live" table in `files/README.md` instead of moving anything.

## 5.4 Check it with them

Show a 5 line summary and ask what's wrong:

> Here's what I've got. Fern & Finch is a 2 person design studio in Bristol, mostly local hospitality and healthcare clients. Priya freelances 3 days a week. Main clients right now: Greenway Café, Marlow Dental, Hollins Lettings. You invoice on the 1st and VAT is quarterly. Your emails are friendly and short, signed "Cheers, Sam". Anything I've got wrong?

Fix what they correct.

## 5.5 Make the app look like their business

Follow `.claude/skills/match-my-brand`: ask for their brand guidelines or website (or 3 quick questions if they have neither), then put their colour, font and logo on the app. If you found their website in 5.1, say so and offer to use it, so they only have to say yes. Show them the result in light and dark before moving on. If they'd rather keep the standard look, that's fine: skip it.

Tick step 5.
