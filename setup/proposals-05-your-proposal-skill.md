# Step 9: your proposal skill

Goal: everything you've learned in setup becomes one skill named after their business, so every proposal comes out the way they'd write it: here, and in Claude everywhere else (including their phone). This is the moment they see Claude learn their business, so make it feel like a gift, not homework.

1. Follow `.claude/skills/build-my-proposal-skill`. It reads their brand, packages, template, voice, delivery and onboarding from what setup saved, asks at most 2 questions, writes the skill, saves it in `.claude/skills/` and packs it into `skills-to-upload/`.
2. **Offer 1 or 2 more skills**, one line each, built the same way and only if they want them (default: yes to the first):
   - **Welcome a new client**: the steps and messages when someone says yes, from "When someone says yes" in `context/proposals.md`.
   - **Answer a quote request**: how they reply to "how much would it cost to...", with their packages and the questions they always ask.
   Name each after the business (`fern-and-finch-welcome`) and follow `setup/06-build-your-skills.md`, section 7.3, for the format and the 2 places they're saved.
3. Tell them how to add the skills to Claude (the words are in `build-my-proposal-skill`, step 4), and offer once to open the **skills-to-upload** folder for them.

Tick step 9 in `setup/progress.md`, noting which skills you built.
