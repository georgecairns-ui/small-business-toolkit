# Step 4 of 5: group by the business, then show the gaps

Goal: every skill sits in the business area it belongs to, and they've seen the Gaps page, the one worth sharing on a call.

## 1. Group the skills

Follow `.claude/skills/group-my-skills`.

## 2. Walk through the Gaps page

Open **http://localhost:4747/skill-map/** and go to Gaps. Walk them through it: which areas already have skills, which have none, and what a skill could do in each empty one (the app shows 3 ideas per area).

## 3. Check the groups

Ask them to check the groups on the Map or All skills page:

> Have a look through the groups. If anything's in the wrong place, open it and change it in the side panel, then press **It looks right** when you're happy.

## 4. Get the first suggestions

Follow `.claude/skills/review-my-skills` once, so the Review page has its first suggestions ready.

## 5. The page to share

Tell them the Gaps page is the one worth sharing on a call with anyone else in the business: it shows what Claude could be doing that nobody's built yet.

End with this plain line, no prices:

> Want these built for you? getaipowers.com

Gaps is for the skills side. The Clean-up page is its twin on the folders side, the one worth sharing when it's time to tidy up.

Tick this step in `setup/progress.md`.
