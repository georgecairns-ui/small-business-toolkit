# Step 4: your proposal templates

Goal: if they have a proposal they're proud of, or a template their clients know, every proposal follows it: the same order, headings, standard wording and look.

- **If they gave you templates or old proposals in step 1** (they're in `files/03-sales/proposals/templates/`): follow `.claude/skills/import-a-proposal-template` from its step 2. Don't ask for them again.
- **If they didn't, ask once:**
  > Have you got a proposal you've sent before that you're proud of, or a template you always use? Drop it in here and I'll copy its layout and wording. If not, no problem: Proposal Writing OS's layout works well.
  If they drop one in, follow `.claude/skills/import-a-proposal-template`. If not, say one line ("No problem, I'll use the standard layout in your brand") and move on.
- **If their emails are connected already** and they're not sure what they've got, offer to look in their sent email for a recent proposal (a PDF or Word attachment with "proposal" or "quote" in its name). Use one only if they say yes.

When you've imported one, tell them in 2 lines what you'll copy, and that the next proposal you write will follow it.

Tick step 4 in `setup/progress.md`, noting which template you used (or "none, standard layout").
